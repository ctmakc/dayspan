Dayspan

Your work day has a fixed size. Hold it in your hands and see what will not fit before 9 a.m.

  1. 01Pinch a tile in the tray and drop it on the span in front of you. Tiles are as long as the time they take.
  2. 02Hold a tile with both hands and pull apart to add time, push together to trim. Fifteen minute steps.
  3. 03Anything past the pink flag is overtime. Trim, or put it back in the tray for tomorrow.
  4. 04Poke Lock day. Pinch-tap tiles as you finish them. Each locked day grows a ring.

Formats: Write proposal 90m, Client call 45 min, Deep work 1h30. Add #focus #meet #admin #life to set a colour, otherwise Dayspan guesses. Everything stays in this browser (localStorage); nothing is sent anywhere.

Mouse preview: drag tiles onto the span, scroll over a tile to resize, click panel buttons.
